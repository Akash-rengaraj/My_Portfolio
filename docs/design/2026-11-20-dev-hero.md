# Developer hero — "the catch" (vector v2)

## Intent

The first thing developers and HR see. In a few seconds of scrolling it should show two things:
a strong engineer, and taste. A client throws a problem away. I catch it, turn it into a spec,
build and test it, and ship it back to the people who needed it. It is one continuous camera
move and never cuts. The spec is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-vector-hero-v2-design.md`.

## Approach

- **All vector, all self-hosted.** One SVG world, with no WebGL, video or AI imagery. It stays
  sharp at any size, loads light, and the CSP is unchanged.
- **Depth:** the scene is split into three parallax layers: far (grid and bokeh, depth 0.6),
  mid (the story, depth 1) and near (dust and blurred foreground, depth 1.35). A 2D camera
  `{x, y, zoom, tilt}` drives all three through `layerTransform`.
- **Paper:** a shaded sheet with a drop shadow, ruled lines and Caveat handwriting that writes
  itself on load. It crumples by polygon morph into a shaded ball with creases. When unfolded,
  the creases stay.
- **Throw:** the ball flies along a curved path with real motion blur (`feGaussianBlur`) and a
  ghost trail. The camera tilts and zooms out, then in.
- **Catch:** my cursor streaks in, then a shockwave and `caught()`. Scroll slows here (`TIME_WARPS`).
- **Interactive catch:** while the ball is in flight, the visitor's pointer becomes a blue
  "you" cursor. Clicking or tapping the ball swaps the caption to "Nice catch. Now watch what I
  do with it." and shows a toast.
- **Analysis → build → ship:** highlights fly into a glass `spec.ts` panel. The code is typed,
  and tests, build and deploy pass. A scan-line wipe reveals the product. The camera pulls back
  as users light up and the client says "finally. it just works ✓". The outro hands off to `#work`.

## Story (story units 0–174; ~1740svh of scroll; time-warped around the catch)

| t       | Beat                                                                                    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–10    | Note on the desk (written on load); name + tagline; camera settles in.                  |
| 10–29   | 01 the throw: scribble, crumple, flight with blur and ghosts; visitor can catch it.     |
| 29–42   | 02 the catch: shockwave, `caught()`, unfold.                                            |
| 42–62   | 03 understand: highlights lift into `problem.md` (symptoms, affected, impact, done).    |
| 62–82   | 04 root cause: five whys down to the ROOT CAUSE tag.                                    |
| 82–98   | 05 SWOT: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.                                 |
| 98–126  | 06 the decision: scored options, the optimal pick, O(n²) → O(n); pull back to the wall. |
| 126–150 | 07 the build: spec → code → tests → deploy wipe → product.                              |
| 150–166 | 08 shipped: users connect and light up, client bubble.                                  |
| 166–174 | Outro: the whole journey in one frame → replay.                                         |

An orange thread of reasoning links the note → every board → the editor.

## Constraints

- The page is statically rendered; the scene is server-rendered at its opening frame and the
  timeline takes over after fonts load.
- Phones (`max-aspect-ratio: 4/5`) get their own camera shots.
- Reduced motion: no pinning, one viewport tall, the note is fully written, and the hint and
  rail are hidden.
- The product shown (OrderSync) is an illustrative story, not a claimed project.

## Files (`src/components/hero/`)

- `geometry.ts`: outlines, PRNG, parallax camera maths, `localToWorld`.
- `camera-path.ts`: `smoothstep`, `windowProgress`, `warpProgress`.
- `scene-layout.ts`: palette, positions, copy and code lines.
- `story-beats.ts`: beats, catch window, time warps.
- `story-rig.ts`: the animated rig, frame sizing and layer transforms.
- `analysis-layout.ts`, `analysis-boards.tsx`, `analysis-timeline.ts`: the investigation wall
  (brief, five whys, SWOT, decision: content, SVG boards, tweens).
- `svg-parts.tsx`: glass panel, title bar, fonts.
- `story-scene.tsx`: the layered SVG scene and the "you" cursor.
- `story-timeline.ts`: the master timeline, render pass, intro writing and overlay tweens.
- `use-catch-interaction.ts`: the visitor catch.
- `dev-hero.tsx`: the section, overlays, ScrollTrigger, resize and reduced motion.
